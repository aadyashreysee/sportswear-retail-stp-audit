# Decoding STP in the Real World: Sportswear Retail Audit

A primary field-research brand positioning audit comparing **Nike, Adidas, and New Balance** within the sports footwear category at **DLF Promenade / Ambience Mall, Vasant Kunj (New Delhi)**.

## Audit Methodology
* **5-Parameter In-Store Observation:** Evaluated price architecture, store design and atmospherics (lighting, music, display density), observed customer demographics and behavior, visual brand communication cues, and structured salesperson interviews.
* **AI-vs-Field Reality Check:** Benchmarked generic AI-generated STP profiles against ground-level store observations to expose where secondary assumptions diverge from live retail execution.
* **Evidence Discipline:** Separated primary field observations from cited secondary supplements (loyalty programs such as Nike Membership and adiClub) rather than smoothing over unrecorded observation fields.

## Cross-Brand Comparison Summary

| Parameter | Nike | Adidas | New Balance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Observed Price Range** | ₹8,495 – ₹19,950 | ₹6,599 – ₹19,000 | ₹9,000 – ₹30,000 (990 series) |
| **Display & Store Feel** | Sparse, curated; sporty & minimal | Packed, dense; use-case organised | Moderate; quiet-luxury & performance |
| **Observed Age Cohort** | 20 – 45 (broadest) | 35 – 45 (oldest observed) | 20 – 30 (youngest observed) |
| **Staff Engagement** | 1-to-1 personal selling | Self-service discovery | High responsiveness & product knowledge |
| **Core Positioning Cue** | Cultural icons (*Air Force 1*, *Air Max*) | Tech & heritage (*Training*, *Hyperboost*) | Verbatim wall tagline: *"No Hype Needed"* |

## Key Insight
While secondary AI profiles assumed New Balance targets older millennials (ages 28–45), live fieldwork revealed the **youngest observed shopper base (ages 20–30)** paying the **highest price ceiling (₹30,000)** for understated, Made-in-UK heritage runners backed by explicit *"No Hype Needed"* signage.
